## §61 evidence and limits

1. **Did loot change a later action?** The artifact records visible loot, comparison/equip or keep decisions, and subsequent target tracks in one normal-fresh-save run. The integrated stress run separately exercises the ordered UI path through fight, loot, comparison, equip, higher ranks, Wolf King reward, and save/reload. This establishes that the system and scripted agent can traverse those links. It does not establish that a gear change caused the later target choice: the helper follows displayed goals, and no counterfactual run or independent player choice isolates that causal link.
2. **Was there a reason to seek elite/Boss rather than repeat the safest target?** UI goal text explicitly named higher-rank comparison and the Wolf King-exclusive Moon Fang reward; the corrected helper selected all five ranks and revisited Wolf King six times. In contrast, after eligible forest population depleted, the agent made 734 legal-rest decisions and the final blocked sample showed all rows disabled at population 0.51. The run shows communicated goals, accessible progression and a real population wait. It does not measure perceived risk, willingness, or whether a person would choose the boss over safer repetition.
3. **Did gear create strategically distinct builds?** Drops/comparisons showed attack, defense/block, critical, penetration and bleed differences; the final active equipment is an Epic multi-affix Moon Fang Spear and Warding armor. Simulations provide separate controlled engine-outcome comparisons. The browser trace does not isolate build causality against matched encounters, and the agent’s automatic stat policy plus item oscillation cannot stand in for a human build choice.
