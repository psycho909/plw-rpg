## Required shared provenance and evidence

At each lane start/end pin HEAD; sorted recursive `src/**` path→SHA-256 map including untracked files; canonical fingerprint `SHA256(JSON.stringify(sortedMap))`; package/lock/build config; Phase 7 spec/tickets/release; runner, launcher and imported helper hashes; build-status hash, source map and exact `dist/index.html`/JS/CSS asset hashes. Verify served HTTP assets equal the frozen production build. Fail attribution if HEAD, source, harness, build or helper changes. Capture UTC and Asia/Taipei timestamps, Node/npm/Vitest/Chromium versions, command/env, true exit status, stdout/stderr, run IDs, actual duration and append-only raw JSONL/checkpoints through `recorded_reports.py`.

Browser metrics: observed content IDs and natural-vs-fixture acquisition; visible route/cue/action/outcome; save/reload persistence; page/console/request/HTTP/storage errors and rejected promises; operations, checkpoint intervals, game days, save latency, localStorage/journal/history/event growth, heap/DOM/listener samples, and duration. Separate product findings (interest/choice/reward feel) from correctness bugs; automation is not human fun/retention evidence.

Simulation/loot metrics: exact seeds, horizons and completed denominators; per-year ecology/population/spawn/boss/cooldown/crisis, inventory/material/craft/crop economy, save/reload and continuation equality; per content stratum generated count, drop/rarity/affix/material/exclusive rate, utility classifications, drought distribution and deterministic mirror hash. Preserve failed and incomplete raw results; do not replace a failure with a retry pass.
