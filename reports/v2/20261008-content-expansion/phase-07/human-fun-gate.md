## Player prompts

After playing naturally for 1–2 hours, ask the player to answer in their own words:

1. What do you most want to do now, and why?
2. Which NPC do you remember, and why?
3. Which world event do you remember, and why?
4. What kind of person is your character now?
5. What feels truly like it belongs to you?
6. Did your actions change the world? Where?
7. If your character died now, what would they leave behind?
8. Do you want to keep playing? If yes, what would you do next? If no, why?

## Interpretation guidance

Read the eight answers together with the observations and the actual play trace. Look for specific personal goals and attachment (“I want to see…”, “that NPC…”, “my farm…”, “next time…”), and for reward, goal, or repetition drought (“I do not know what to do”, “nothing in particular”, “I just keep repeating…”). Do not reduce the result to a yes/no score, and do not infer fun, retention, or product approval from an Agent/Playwright session.
