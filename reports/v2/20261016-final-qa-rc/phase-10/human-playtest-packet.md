## Session protocol

- **Duration:** 1–2 hours of normal play, using the production build and an ordinary fresh save. Do not inject resources, jump world time, or use debug controls. If a controlled save is needed to observe a rare event, stop the normal-play session first and record it separately; do not combine its observations with the normal route.
- **Before starting:** record the build URL or artifact, source commit, seed, date, browser/OS/device, viewport, and participant code. Confirm the participant understands the session, what notes are collected, how long they are retained, and that they may stop at any time. Obtain consent before starting. Do not record names or other identifying details in this packet.
- **During play:** observe without coaching or suggesting goals. Record the elapsed real play time and notable moments in the blank log below. Ask the eight questions verbatim at the end; record the participant’s own words without interpretation. Ask permission before audio/video recording; otherwise use written notes only.
- **After play:** keep the raw notes separate from analysis. Redact accidental identifying details. The facilitator may add a clearly labeled analysis section only after preserving the participant’s verbatim responses.
