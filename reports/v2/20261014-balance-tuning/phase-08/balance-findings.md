# Phase8-F Consolidated Balance Findings

## Reading rules

Normal fresh-save evidence is scripted-policy output, not a human-player probability. Controlled combat, controlled loot, controlled economy probes, and controlled world parameter variants answer different questions and are never merged into route frequencies. “NOT OBSERVED” does not mean impossible; “NOT MODELED” does not imply a defect. No new target rates are introduced here.
